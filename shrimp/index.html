    New tank: cycle it first

    A new tank has no filter bacteria, so waste has nowhere to go and shrimp added now will mostly die. Add ammonia to feed the bacteria. One group eats ammonia and makes nitrite, a second eats nitrite and makes nitrate. Add more only when ammonia drops near zero, until both bacteria bars reach 100% and ammonia and nitrite read zero. That takes three to four weeks. Too much ammonia burns through KH; when KH hits zero the pH falls and the cycle stalls. A water change fixes it.
