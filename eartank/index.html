Ear tank

A tank you keep by ear. Phone speaker is fine, mono. Tap anything to start the sound.

What to test, eyes closed

  1. Feed, then skip two days. Can you tell fed from hungry?
  2. Run a fortnight at a week a minute. Can you hear the water getting old, and the change fixing it?
  3. 6 shrimp, 14, 30. Can you tell roughly how many?
  4. A molt, a berried female, babies, one death. Do you notice them without being told?
  5. Leave it on 20 minutes. Do you want to turn it off?
  6. A tank you built and one you adopted. Do they sound different?

The sounds

Bubbles are the filter. Each tick is a shrimp, high is small, low is big. Hungry ticks are thin and quick. A rustle is moss, a knock is wood, a clink is rock. A slide down is a molt. A double tick is a female with eggs. A thin hiss is bad water, change it. When everything goes dull the water is old. One low tap is a death.
